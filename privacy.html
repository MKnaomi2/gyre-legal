Privacy Policy — GYRE

Effective / last updated: 2026-09-28
App: GYRE (iOS)
Publisher: Wadevo Studios
Bundle ID: com.mknaomi2.gyre

This policy describes what GYRE does with information on your device. GYRE keeps progress and settings on-device only. It does not create accounts, and it does not include analytics or advertising SDKs in the current source.

Summary
  • GYRE stores your progress and preferences on your device only.
  • GYRE does not create accounts or require sign-in.
  • GYRE does not include advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, or social SDKs in the current source.
  • GYRE does not track you across apps or websites for advertising (privacy manifest: NSPrivacyTracking = false).
  • You can erase local progress from Settings → Data → Reset Progress.

What we store on your device

GYRE uses Apple’s UserDefaults (declared in PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy with reason CA92.1) to keep a small on-device JSON blob that may include:

  • Stage clear status, stars, best scores / precision records, and cosmetic unlock / loadout choices
  • Feel settings: sound, haptics, reduce flash, reduce motion
  • Related local progression fields needed to resume play after relaunch

This data never leaves your device through GYRE’s own code paths as audited (no URLSession / network client usage found in Sources).

What we do not collect

As of the audited source and privacy manifest:

  • No contact information, location, identifiers for advertising, usage analytics, diagnostics uploads, or purchase history collected by GYRE
  • No account registration, login, or cloud sync
  • Empty NSPrivacyCollectedDataTypes and empty NSPrivacyTrackingDomains in PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy

App Store Connect label: choose Data Not Collected only if the final Release archive still matches this policy. Do not invent network or analytics features to justify other labels.

Reset / deletion

There is no cloud account to delete. To remove local game progress:

  1. Open Settings in GYRE
  2. Under Data, tap Reset Progress and confirm

Feel settings are intentionally preserved across reset (sound / haptics / accessibility toggles). Uninstalling the app also removes on-device UserDefaults for GYRE.

Permissions and system features

  • Haptics / sound: controlled in Settings; no special privacy permission prompts beyond normal AV playback.
  • Reduce Motion: respects the in-app toggle and, on iOS, the system Reduce Motion preference.
  • GYRE does not use App Tracking Transparency (ATT) because it does not track.

Children

GYRE is a single-player arcade game with no social features and no data collection as described above. Age rating and Kids Category decisions are App Store Connect questionnaire items for the publisher; this policy does not claim Kids Category eligibility by itself.

Support and contact

Questions about privacy or the app:

  • Support: support@wadevo.com
  • Privacy: privacy@wadevo.com
  • In-app: Settings → About links to this policy and a mailto support address.

If you contact us by email, the message content and your email address are processed only to respond to you (ordinary email correspondence, not in-app telemetry).

Changes

If GYRE later adds networking, accounts, analytics, or other data practices, this policy and the App Store privacy labels will be updated before those features ship. Until then, treat “on-device only” as the product rule.
